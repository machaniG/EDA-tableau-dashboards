# deep-EDA-and-dashboards
This project identifies key drivers of profitability, regional dominance, and counter-intuitive seasonal trends

## Overview 

A retail client had four years of sales data but no structured way to answer basic business questions. I conducted multi-dimensional exploratory data analysis across four analytical lenses simultaneously;  product category, individual SKU, geography, and time.

The client got two valuable insights with immediate operational value. Demand dropped by up to 78% in certain product categories between peak and trough months, directly informing how much stock to hold and when. And profit varied by £0.72M across the top five regions, revealing where marketing spend  should be allocated.

These findings were delivered as three interactive Tableau dashboards their team could explore independently, filter by year, and use in planning meetings without needing a data analyst in the room.
